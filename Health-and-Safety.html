Health & safety

Safe work.
Shared responsibility.

Protecting our employees, clients, contractors, residents and the public is central to every project.

Our commitment

Safety at
every stage.

At AM Plumbing & Heating Ltd, the health, safety and well-being of our employees, clients, contractors, residents and the general public are of utmost importance.

We pride ourselves on maintaining an exemplary health and safety track record, supported by stringent policies, rigorous procedures and a proactive approach on every project.

Complying with regulations is our starting point. We aim to exceed industry standards through best practices and continuous improvement at every level of our business.

Gas Safe registered

Your safety.
Our priority.

As Gas Safe registered engineers, we meet the legal requirements and industry standards for gas work, helping keep homes and businesses safe and compliant.

Check our Gas Safe registration

A culture of collective responsibility

Our approach extends beyond compliance, with particular care around gas handling and associated hazards. We encourage every team member to take ownership of safety.

  • Regular inductions for employees and contractors
  • Ongoing safety training and professional development
  • Frequent toolbox talks and site inspections
  • Open communication to report safety concerns
