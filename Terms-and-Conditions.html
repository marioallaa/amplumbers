7. Changes to system pressure and flow

Some jobs change the pressure or flow in your pipework, for example converting from tank-fed to mains-pressure hot water, fitting an unvented cylinder, a combination boiler, a pump or a shower booster. Higher pressure puts extra stress on every part of the system that stays in use, especially older fittings, joints, valves and concealed pipework, and an old component can fail under the new conditions even when the new work is sound.

  • Before such work we will recommend that you consider inspecting or replacing old, accessible pipework and fittings (see section 6). Replacement is not included unless the Quote says so.
  • Components that remain in use stay at your risk as set out in section 6, subject to your legal rights.
  • Unvented hot water storage must comply with the Building Regulations and the manufacturer’s instructions. We will notify the work as required, and you must have the system serviced as the manufacturer requires and keep the records.

8. Testing, commissioning and records

Before handing the Work back we test and commission it to a standard appropriate to the installation, the manufacturer’s instructions and the regulations that apply, for example by pressure or tightness testing. We record the key details in our job record (date, test type, pressure and duration, which parts were tested, who carried it out and the result), and you can have a copy on request.

A passed test shows that the part tested held at the test conditions at that time. It does not guarantee the condition of parts we did not install or test, or how any part will behave in the future, and it cannot reveal defects that only appear with time or changes in pressure, temperature or use. Where we isolate or leave out part of a system from a test, we will say so.

We may photograph the condition of the property and Existing Installation before, during and after the Work for our records (see our Privacy Policy).

10. After the Work: checking for leaks and empty properties

  • Know your stopcock. Make sure you know where to turn the water off.
  • Check in the first week. After Work on water or heating systems, especially if system pressure has changed, we recommend that you check visible pipework, joints and ceilings below for drips, damp or staining each day for the first week and after any change in use.
  • Empty or unoccupied property. If the property will be empty soon after the Work (holiday, void period, between tenants), please tell us before we finish, and we recommend that you turn the water off at the stopcock while it is empty or arrange for someone to check it regularly. Check your own insurance for any unoccupied-property conditions.
  • If you find a leak: turn the water off at the stopcock, turn off electricity to affected areas if it is safe to do so, call 999 if there is danger to anyone, then call us straight away on +44 7838 628381 and tell your insurer.
